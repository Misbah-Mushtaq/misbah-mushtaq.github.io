Delete your Noor account

This page explains how to delete your Noor account (published by Misbah Mushtaq & Muneeb Ur Rehman) and the data associated with it.

Noor is local-first — you can use it without an account, and most of your data stays on your device. You only have an account if you signed in with your email to sync across devices. Here's how to delete it.

How to request account deletion

  1. Email us at misbahmushtaq75@gmail.com from the email address you use to sign in to Noor.
  2. Use the subject line “Delete my Noor account.” No other details are required.
  3. We verify the request and permanently delete your account within 30 days, then email you to confirm.

You can also remove everything stored on your device at any time, without contacting us — just uninstall the app, or clear its data in your phone's Settings → Apps → Noor → Storage.

Delete some data without closing your account

If you'd rather keep your account but remove specific synced data (for example, your prayer logs or streaks), email misbahmushtaq75@gmail.com and tell us what to remove. We'll delete it within 30 days and leave your account active.

What is deleted

  • Your account and email address.
  • All data synced to our servers under your account — prayer logs, Qada records, streaks, and settings.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Account & synced data: permanently deleted within 30 days of your request; residual copies in encrypted backups are purged within 90 days.
  • On-device data: never sent to us unless you enabled sync — it is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Anonymous analytics: aggregated usage statistics that do not identify you may be retained; they contain no personal information and cannot be tied back to you.

Questions? Email misbahmushtaq75@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
